# 🏙️ CityTwin AI

> **Don't just monitor the city. Simulate it, predict it, and make better decisions before problems happen.**

CityTwin AI is an intelligent **Digital Twin and Predictive City Management Platform** that creates a virtual representation of a city to monitor urban conditions, simulate "what-if" scenarios, predict potential problems, identify root causes, and recommend proactive actions.

Unlike traditional smart-city dashboards that only display current data, CityTwin AI focuses on **understanding what could happen next** and helping authorities make better decisions before real-world problems occur.

---

## 🚀 Why CityTwin AI?

Modern cities generate massive amounts of data from traffic, water systems, electricity networks, environmental sensors, infrastructure, and emergency services. However, these systems are often monitored separately, making it difficult to understand how one problem can affect the rest of the city.

For example:

**Heavy Rainfall**
↓  
**Drainage Overload**
↓  
**Road Flooding**
↓  
**Traffic Diversion**
↓  
**Emergency Response Delay**

CityTwin AI brings these relationships together inside a single virtual city environment.

---

## 💡 Key Features

### 🗺️ Interactive Digital Twin

A virtual representation of the city containing:

- Roads
- Buildings
- Water networks
- Power infrastructure
- Hospitals
- Emergency stations
- City zones
- Critical infrastructure

Multiple visualization layers allow users to monitor different city systems.

---

### 🔮 What-If City Simulator

Simulate possible future scenarios before implementing real-world decisions.

Examples:

- 🌧️ Increased rainfall
- 🚗 Traffic increase
- 🚧 Road closure
- 💧 Water pipeline failure
- ⚡ Increased power demand
- 🌡️ Temperature increase
- 🏗️ Infrastructure failure
- 🚨 Emergency events

The system predicts how these changes could affect the rest of the city.

---

### 🤖 AI Prediction Engine

Identify potential problems before they become critical.

The system can generate predictions such as:

> ⚠️ High probability of flooding in Zone 4

> ⚡ Transformer T12 may become overloaded

> 🚗 Road R5 has increasing congestion risk

Each prediction includes:

- Risk score
- Probability
- Affected location
- Contributing factors
- Expected impact
- Recommended action
